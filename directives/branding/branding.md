# Hestia — Brand Identity

## Name

**Hestia** — Greek goddess of the hearth, the center of every home and every city.

### Why Hestia

- The hearth was the operational center of the Greek household — everything revolved around it. Hestia is the operational center of the brokerage.
- Hestia kept things running without seeking attention. She was the first Olympian but the most understated — she didn't compete for glory, she just made sure the fire stayed lit. The platform isn't flashy new software. It's the quiet infrastructure that makes everything work.
- Every Greek city had a public hearth dedicated to Hestia — it represented the bonds that held the community together. Unified operations.
- The "home" resonance is subtle but relevant — we serve real estate. Hestia is the goddess of the home. The connection sits beneath the surface, exactly where the product sits.

### Name Usage Rules

- Always capitalize: **Hestia**, never "hestia" or "HESTIA"
- Never abbreviate or use acronyms
- In running copy, use "Hestia" on first reference, then "Hestia" or "the platform" / "the system" on subsequent references
- Never pair with "the" as a title (not "The Hestia", just "Hestia")

## Color Palette

### Primary

| Role       | Hex       | Usage                                    |
|------------|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| Background | `#FFFEFC` | Page backgrounds, cards, containers      |
| Foreground | `#07070A` | Headlines, body text, primary UI text    |

### Secondary

| Role       | Hex       | Usage                                    |
|------------|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| Brand Red  | `#A30000` | Logo, primary accent, buttons, CTAs, links |
| Charcoal   | `#504E56` | Subtext, secondary text, muted labels    |

### Accent

| Role       | Hex       | Usage                                    |
|------------|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| Gold       | `#D58936` | Sparingly — highlights, badges, premium indicators. Do not overuse. |

### Extended Palette (derived)

| Token       | Hex       | Usage                                   |
|-------------|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| Red Light   | `#FDF2F2` | Light red tint for backgrounds, cards    |
| Red Hover   | `#8A0000` | Button hover states, active states       |
| Red Dark    | `#700000` | Pressed states, dark UI elements         |
| Border      | `#E8E2DB` | Card borders, dividers, input borders    |
| Muted BG    | `#F0EBE4` | Secondary backgrounds, table stripes     |

### Color Rules

- Background is warm off-white (`#FFFEFC`), never pure white (`#FFFFFF`)
- Primary text is near-black (`#07070A`), never pure black (`#000000`)
- Red (`#A30000`) is the accent — buttons, links, brand marks. Never use as a background for large areas
- Charcoal (`#504E56`) is for secondary/supporting text only
- Gold (`#D58936`) is reserved for special emphasis — use no more than 1-2 instances per page
- Never use green, blue, or purple as accent colors

## Typography

### Fonts

| Role      | Font               | Fallback                    |
|-----------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
| All       | Inter              | system-ui, sans-serif       |

### Type Rules

- Inter everywhere — clean, modern, tech-forward. No serif fonts.
- Headlines differentiate through weight (bold/extrabold) and size, not font family
- The brand should feel like a future-proof AI integration platform, not an old-school legacy system. Typography choices reinforce this — Inter signals modern SaaS, not traditional finance.
- Minimum body size: 14px (0.875rem)
- Line height: 1.5 for body, 1.2 for headlines
- Letter spacing: tight (-0.025em) for headlines, normal for body

## Tone of Voice

- **Conversational, peer-to-peer.** Write like a fellow operator, not a vendor.
- **Direct, no fluff.** Say what it does. Say what it costs. Say how long it takes.
- **Technical competence without jargon.** Show you understand their systems without making them feel dumb.
- **Validate skepticism before presenting solutions.** Acknowledge that they've been burned before.
- **Use industry-specific language.** GCI, production volume, splits, cap structure — speak David's language.

### Never Say

- "Revolutionary" / "game-changing" / "disruptive"
- "Synergy" / "leverage" (as buzzwords — "operational leverage" is fine in context)
- "AI-powered" as a lead — lead with the outcome, mention AI as implementation
- "Free" as a standalone value prop
- "Get your first agents" / "start a brokerage" (below ICP)

## Logo

- **Primary mark:** Hestia wordmark in Brand Red (`#A30000`)
- **Icon:** Keystone shape — the structural stone at the top of a hearth arch that holds everything together. Ties to the name (Hestia → hearth → keystone), the product (the piece that holds disconnected tools together), and works as a clean standalone geometric mark.
- Minimum clear space: height of the "H" on all sides
- On dark backgrounds: use `#FFFEFC` (off-white) wordmark
- On light backgrounds: use `#A30000` (red) wordmark
- Never place the logo on busy/patterned backgrounds
- Never stretch, rotate, or alter the logo proportions

### Logo Rationale

The keystone avoids the obvious (a flame icon would be the expected choice for "Hestia"). Instead it rewards curiosity — people who know the connection see the layered meaning, while everyone else sees a clean geometric mark. The shape is simple, works at any size (favicon, nav bar, app icon, business card), and doesn't require detail to be recognizable.

## Application

### Website / Landing Pages

- Light theme: `#FFFEFC` background, `#07070A` headlines, `#504E56` subtext, `#A30000` accent
- Cards and sections use subtle borders (`#E8E2DB`) for separation
- CTAs use solid `#A30000` background with `#FFFEFC` text
- Form inputs: `#FFFEFC` background with `#E8E2DB` border, `#07070A` input text
- Focus states: 2px ring in `#A30000`

### Portal (App)

- Follows the same palette as website
- Sidebar: `#07070A` or `#FFFEFC` depending on mode
- Active states and selections use `#A30000` accent
- Charts and data visualizations can use the full palette including gold

### Email

- Plain text preferred for cold outreach
- HTML emails for nurture sequences use the same light theme
- Unsubscribe link always present
- Sender name: personal name, not "Hestia Team"

## Brand Positioning

### What Hestia Is

Hestia is a unified business operations platform that connects existing tools (CRM, transaction management, commissions) into one system with AI-powered automation. It is not a replacement — it is the integration layer that makes everything work together.

### Positioning Statement

For independent broker-owners running 15–80 agents who are losing top producers to tech-forward competitors and drowning in disconnected tools, Hestia is the operational infrastructure layer that unifies their existing stack, automates their SOPs, and gives their agents a portal that competes with Compass — live in 30 days, without replacing anything.

### Brand Feel

- **Future-proof, not legacy.** We are an AI operations platform, not a mainframe. Every visual and verbal choice should signal forward motion.
- **Warm, not corporate.** The hearth metaphor is about warmth, community, and keeping things running — not about cold efficiency.
- **Peer, not vendor.** The brand speaks to broker-owners as a fellow operator, not a salesperson.
- **Quiet confidence.** We don't need to shout. The product speaks for itself. The brand reflects that.

### Names Considered

Nexus, Locus, Keystone, Fulcrum, Trellis, Cyrus, Argus, Tycho — ultimately chose Hestia for the layered meaning (hearth, home, operational center, quiet infrastructure), domain availability, and distinctiveness in the real estate industry.
